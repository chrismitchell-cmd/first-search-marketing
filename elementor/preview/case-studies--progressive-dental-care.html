The client

Meet Progressive Dental Care.

Progressive Dental Care is a dental practice in South Portland, Maine, with a strong, long-standing team and patients who love them. When new owners took over the practice, they set out to make it more modern and more comfortable for patients, without losing the people and values that made it special.

Where they were

During the ownership change, the practice hired an agency that specialized in “transitions”. The results never came. New patients still arrived almost entirely through referrals, the brand looked dated next to the practice the new owners were building, and online they were hard to find: an average map pack position of #17, 75 Google reviews and about 12 digital leads a month. At the end of 2025, they came to us.
