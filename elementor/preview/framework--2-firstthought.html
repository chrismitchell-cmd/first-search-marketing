What is the difference between FirstBrand and FirstThought?

FirstBrand builds the foundation: who you are, how you look and sound, your website and the tracking behind it. FirstThought puts that foundation in front of the people searching for what you sell. A strong brand nobody finds doesn’t grow, and visibility for a weak brand doesn’t convert, which is why the stages work as a system.
